How fatigue is monitored

Session load is estimated as duration in minutes × session RPE (1–10). BETTER compares the latest seven days with your average weekly load across 28 days and considers your daily fatigue, sleep and leg-freshness check-ins. These are simple personal indicators, not validated clinical thresholds or injury predictions. Use judgement; pain, illness or unusual exhaustion are reasons to stop and seek appropriate advice.

How to interpret this

Use trends to prompt a review, not to diagnose fatigue or predict injury. The 7-day load divided by the 28-day weekly average is descriptive only; it is not a validated safe/unsafe threshold. Missing sessions and inaccurate RPE values affect the picture.
